# Microsoft Teams Recording Not Showing: Troubleshooting Guide

Microsoft Teams meeting recordings help employees review discussions, revisit important decisions, and share information with colleagues. However, sometimes a meeting ends and the expected recording does not appear.

A missing recording does not necessarily mean the video has been deleted. It may still be processing, stored in a different Microsoft 365 location, or inaccessible because of account permissions.

This guide covers practical checks to help locate missing Teams recordings.

## 1. Check the Meeting Chat

Start by opening the chat associated with the meeting. Look for a recording notification or link that may appear after the recording has finished processing.

Make sure you are checking the correct meeting conversation, especially if your calendar contains multiple meetings with similar names.

## 2. Check OneDrive and SharePoint

Microsoft Teams recordings are generally stored in Microsoft 365 cloud storage.

- **Regular meetings:** Recordings are commonly saved in the meeting organizer's OneDrive, often in a folder named Recordings.
- **Channel meetings:** Recordings are generally stored in the team's SharePoint site.

Search the relevant location using the meeting date or recording name. The exact location and access depend on the meeting type and organizational configuration.

## 3. Allow Time for Recording Processing

A recording may not become available immediately after a meeting ends. Processing and uploading can take time depending on the recording and service conditions.

Wait a little before checking again. Revisit the meeting chat and the relevant OneDrive or SharePoint location.

## 4. Verify Account and File Permissions

If the recording exists but cannot be opened, confirm that you are signed in with the correct work or school account.

If another person organized the meeting, ask them to check the file's sharing permissions. For channel recordings, SharePoint site access may also affect availability.

## 6. Prevent Future Recording Access Problems

Before important meetings, confirm who is organizing the session and where the recording is expected to be stored.

After the meeting:

- Check the meeting chat for the recording link.
- Allow time for processing.
- Verify the relevant OneDrive or SharePoint location.
- Confirm that intended viewers have access.

## Conclusion

When a Microsoft Teams recording is missing, check the meeting chat, cloud storage location, processing status, and file permissions. These steps can help identify the issue before assuming that the recording has been lost.
